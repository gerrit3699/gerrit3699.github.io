<section class="page-section section-attractor">
  <h2>Before you ask me</h2>

  <p>
    I appreciate serious messages.
    What I currently cannot do, and also do not want to do,
    is explain from the beginning to each person again and again in individual conversations
    what Semantic Gravitation is,
    why it could work,
    or how each building block is derived in detail.
  </p>

  <p>
    If you really want to understand what this is about,
    please first use the materials on this website:
    especially the documents, papers, and patents on the corresponding pages.
  </p>

  <p>
    My suggestion is:
    take your real question,
    upload the documents into an AI of your choice,
    work your way into the material yourself,
    and examine it at the point
    that truly interests, irritates, or calls you.
  </p>

  <p>
    Do not believe too quickly.
    Do not reject too quickly.
    But ask precisely,
    examine carefully,
    and make your own short-circuit
    the starting point
    of the next better question.
  </p>

  <p>
    Anyone who truly wants to enter can begin:
    with the documents,
    with the questions,
    and with AI as a workspace and examination space.
  </p>
</section>
